# Demelza Hue Labyrinth

## 📝 Instructions
* At each level, you will be presented with a grid of colored geometric shapes (starting with 10 shapes and increasing up to 16 shapes by level 10).
* Each shape will pulse, glow, and play a unique musical note in a coded sequence.
* When the **'ENTER CODE SEQUENCE'** message displays, click or tap the shapes in the exact same order as the coded sequence.
* If your entry matches the sequence, it will replay with an additional step added to the end.
* Continue entering the sequence correctly until you complete the level and move to the next one.
* If you enter the sequence incorrectly at any time, the level will end, and you must restart it from the beginning.

**Good Luck!**

## ✨ Features
* **10 Challenging Levels:** The game gets progressively harder with longer sequences and more shapes.
* **Dynamic Geometry:** Features a variety of shapes (circles, triangles, stars, hexagons, etc.) instead of just standard discs.
* **Musical Feedback:** Each shape plays a soft, melodic tone when clicked (Web Audio API).
* **Calming Background Music:** A continuous, gentle piano arpeggio plays in the background (can be toggled ON/OFF).
* **Interactive Background:** A dark, tech-themed background with roaming robots. Hover over or tap a robot to pause it and read a fun fact about Artificial Intelligence.
* **Fully Responsive:** Designed to fit perfectly on both desktop and mobile screens without any scrolling required.

## 🎮 Controls
* **Mouse / Touch:** Click or tap the shapes to enter the code.
* **Music Button:** Toggle the background piano music ON or OFF.
* **Main Menu:** Return to the start menu at any time.
* **Restart Level 1:** Instantly reset your progress back to the very first level.
